# User guide — release 6.0.0-ui

Sign in with the account the administrator gave you, and change the temporary password when asked. The header shows the active project, your category and your role. If you work on several projects, switch between them there. The left menu depends on your role; on a phone, open it with the ☰ button.

## Contractor (Category 3)
1. **New DOC_01:** check the project and contract, enter the batch reference, then choose *Create DOC_01 and add RFI rows*.
2. **+ Add RFI row** for each RFI: RFI No., description, station from/to, side, material source and offset, inspection date and time, and remark. Fields marked * are required before submission. Rows are saved as drafts as you add them; *Save draft* keeps the row you are editing.
3. Add DOC_01 enclosures (prior test results and so on) at the bottom of the page.
4. **Submit DOC_01.** The confirmation shows the project, batch, number of rows and every requested inspection time, and warns about short notice (under 24 hours). After submitting, the rows and enclosures are locked. Corrections need an amendment approved by the RE/DRE.
5. **Draft Submissions** lists drafts and batches returned to you, with the reason. **Submitted Batches** and **My RFI Records** let you follow progress. *Resubmit (next revision)* appears when an RFI is "Not Approved" or partly approved.

## RE / DRE (Category 2.1)
- **Dashboard:** new DOC_01 batches with the next action, RFIs under review, RFIs awaiting your decision, and completions this week. Short-notice batches are marked in amber.
- **DOC_01 Submissions → batch:**
  - *Acknowledge receipt*.
  - Write a **management remark** and attach files or photographs to it, then *Record remark*.
  - *Return to contractor*: give a reason and optionally attach marked-up files.
- **Inspector Assignment → Assign:** add inspectors in the order they must review. Write a remark and attach supporting files if needed, then *Save assignment*. Once the first review is in, changing the sequence requires a reason.
- **Workflow Monitor:** the timeline shows each stage: who, role, when, comments and evidence. The active inspector shows a due time; pending inspectors are greyed out. Select a stage to see its details.
- **Final Decisions → Decide:**
  - Read every inspector's comment and evidence.
  - Select **one** of the six DOC_03 dispositions. A partial approval needs the executed section.
  - Write your final remark and attach files if needed.
  - *Record disposition*. The confirmation shows exactly what will be recorded. The record is then locked and the DOC_03 is issued.
- **Corrections:** approve or reject DOC_01 amendments and evidence corrections on the RFI record.

Your remarks and files belong to you. A colleague cannot record them with their own action, and nobody else sees your drafts.

## Inspectors (Category 2.2)
- **My Assigned RFIs** shows *Your turn is active* (open the review) and *Awaiting your turn* (who is reviewing now).
- **DOC_03 review:**
  - Write your comment. It saves automatically and stays on the device if the connection drops.
  - Add evidence with **Camera** (take a photo), **Gallery** or **Files**. You can see each upload's progress, cancel it, and *Retry* if the network fails; retrying never creates a duplicate. *Remove* a file before you submit if needed.
  - **Submit review**: check the confirmation (RFI, your role, your place in the sequence, comment, number of files, your name, time). Your review and files are then locked and the next inspector is notified.
- If you could not attach files because camera permission was denied, use *Gallery* or *Files*.
- A submitted review is read-only. If something was wrong, use *Request a correction*: give the reason and the corrected text and/or a replacement file. The RE/DRE approves it, and the original is kept.
- **Review History** and **Attachments** list your past reviews and your files.

## Office (Category 2.3)
- **DOC_02 Register:**
  - Filter by RFI No., description, road type, RFI status and inspection dates; *More filters* gives the rest.
  - Sort by clicking column headers (keyboard: Enter).
  - Choose columns, and save the result as a view.
  - Where permitted, show the workbook's hidden fields I:M and S.
  - Export to Excel or PDF. Exports contain only what you can see.
  - Historical documents that were on the former shared drive show "File unavailable — migration required".
- **Completed DOC_03:** shows each section, the disposition and the attachment index. *Open completed DOC_03 PDF* returns the document exactly as issued. *Download evidence bundle* gives a ZIP with the PDF, the original files and a manifest (author, stage, time, checksum).
- **Search** and **Exports** give quick access to the register.
